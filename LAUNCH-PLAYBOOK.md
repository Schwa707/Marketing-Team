## Launch Positioning

> **For business owners facing closure, downsizing, or surplus inventory who need to recover capital fast without the chaos of fire-sale auctions, ConsignSource is a private liquidation matching network that connects you with pre-vetted buyers, auctioneers, and asset recovery partners best suited to your specific assets and timeline. Unlike posting blindly on equipment marketplaces or cold-calling auction houses, we run a discreet, advisor-led process that protects your reputation and maximizes recovery.**

**Core message pillars (use across every channel):**
1. **Discretion** — "private liquidation," not a public going-out-of-business signal
2. **Speed** — capital recovery in weeks, not quarters
3. **Match quality** — right buyer for the asset class, not the loudest auctioneer
4. **Advisor-led** — a human (you) curates, not a search box

**Anti-positioning** (what we are NOT — say this out loud):
- Not a public auction site
- Not a classified listings board
- Not a foreclosure / distressed-asset speculator
- Not "another platform" — a curated network

## Week-by-Week Plan

### Week 1 — Foundation & Supply-Side Recruitment

**Objective:** Lock positioning. Recruit first 10 liquidator/buyer partners from your existing LinkedIn network.

- [ ] **Lock the positioning statement** above (or refine in one sitting — don't iterate forever)
- [ ] **One-pager** (1 page Google Doc) — internal alignment doc covering: who we serve, who we don't, fee model, partner criteria, launch goal
- [ ] **Landing page live** (Carrd, Framer, or Webflow — $19–25/yr) with:
  - Headline: *"Liquidating a business? Get matched with the right buyers — privately."*
  - 3-bullet value prop (discretion, speed, match quality)
  - Two CTAs: **"Get a Confidential Assessment"** (seller form) + **"Join the Buyer Network"** (provider form)
  - Email capture wired to your email tool
- [ ] **Email tool** — MailerLite free tier (up to 1,000 contacts, free) OR ConvertKit free tier. Set up:
  - Seller pre-launch list
  - Buyer/partner pre-launch list
  - Referral-partner list (attorneys, brokers, CPAs)
- [ ] **LinkedIn profile audit** — your headline, About, and Featured section must telegraph ConsignSource. Update headline to: *"Helping business owners liquidate quickly and privately | Founder, ConsignSource"*
- [ ] **Sales Navigator trial** ($99/mo — keep through Week 7 minimum) — for prospecting referral partners and liquidator/buyer partners
- [ ] **Tracking** — Plausible (free trial / $9/mo) or GA4 (free); UTM parameters on every LinkedIn link
- [ ] **Buyer-side outreach list (Tier 1: warm intros only)** — 30 names from your network: regional auction houses, equipment dealers, asset recovery firms, used-equipment brokers, secondary inventory buyers, scrap/recycling brokers by category
- [ ] **Personally DM 15 of those Tier-1 buyers** with a soft ask: *"Building a private liquidation matching network — would you be open to a 15-min call about being one of our launch partners?"*

**Deliverables end of Week 1:**
- Positioning locked
- Landing page live with two capture flows
- 15 buyer-partner conversations booked or pending

### Week 2 — Demand-Side Discovery & Content Engine Setup

**Objective:** Build the LinkedIn content engine. Validate seller pain via 5 customer-discovery calls.

- [ ] **5 customer-discovery calls** with business owners in your network who've liquidated (or are considering it). Ask:

  - What did you try first?
  - What was the worst part of the process?
  - How did you find the buyer/auctioneer?
  - What would have made it easier?
  - **Verbatim quotes from these calls become your best LinkedIn copy.**

  5. **Behind-the-scenes** — "Day 8 of building ConsignSource"

- [ ] **Batch-write 20 LinkedIn posts** (4 weeks of content at 5/week) — use the templates in [Social Media Content](#social-media-content) below
- [ ] **Set up Buffer free tier or LinkedIn native scheduling** for the next 4 weeks
- [ ] **LinkedIn Newsletter** — start one called *"The Liquidation Insider"* or similar. Weekly. Free distribution amplifier.
- [ ] **Referral partner list (Tier 2: warm intros to professionals)** — 50 names: bankruptcy attorneys, business brokers, CPAs serving SMBs, commercial real estate brokers handling lease exits, equipment finance lenders, factoring companies. These send you sellers.
- [ ] **Sign first 5 buyer-side partners** from Week 1 conversations — partner agreement is one page, no exclusivity, just a signed willingness to evaluate matched deals

**Deliverables end of Week 2:**

## Post-Launch Plan (Week 8 → Month 3)

### Week 8 Retrospective Framework
For each section, write 3 bullets, no more:
1. **Goal vs Actual** — Did we hit 40 inquiries / 15 partners?
2. **Channel performance** — What % of inquiries came from LinkedIn organic vs sponsored vs referral?
3. **Email performance** — Open / click / conversion by email
4. **Top-converting content** — Which 3 LinkedIn posts drove the most landing-page traffic?
5. **Top objections** — What are people actually worried about?
6. **What worked** — 3 things to double down on
7. **What didn't** — 3 things to kill
8. **Unexpected insights** — What surprised us?
9. **Next 30 days** — 3 specific commitments

### Sustainable Cadence (Month 2+)
- **LinkedIn:** 3x/week posts + biweekly Newsletter (down from 5x daily during launch)
- **Referral-partner program:** monthly "deal flow update" email + quarterly virtual roundtable
- **Buyer-partner program:** weekly digest of new matches by category
- **Content:** introduce a monthly long-form case study (anonymized closed deal)
- **Paid:** continue boosting top-performing organic only — no cold ads at this budget

### Common Launch Mistakes to Avoid (industry-specific)
1. **Launching without buyer supply locked.** A seller submits and you have nothing to match them with = trust destroyed. Recruit supply first.
2. **Over-publicizing case studies.** Discretion is your moat. Anonymize aggressively or get explicit written permission.
3. **Pricing surprises.** State success-fee ranges publicly. Sellers who feel bait-and-switched will torch you in reviews.
4. **Slow response time.** Distressed sellers move on if you don't reply within 4 business hours. Calendar discipline matters more than fancy automations.
5. **Confusing "consignment" framing.** The brand is ConsignSource, but to most business owners, "liquidation" or "asset recovery" is the search term. Lead with their language; their term wins.
